# Google Antigravity (the IDE and the agy CLI) – conversation transcripts
# Syntax: see claude_code.txt.
#
# Every conversation keeps a plaintext transcript in its "brain" folder, in up
# to three copies; SynthSift reads the fullest and skips the others:
#   brain/<conversation id>/.system_generated/logs/transcript_full.jsonl
#   brain/<conversation id>/.system_generated/logs/transcript.jsonl   (large outputs truncated)
#   brain/<conversation id>/.system_generated/logs/overview.txt
# The agent's own store, conversations/<id>.pb (AES-encrypted) or .db
# (protobuf in an unpublished schema), cannot be read and is not collected.

# The IDE: ~/.gemini/antigravity, or antigravity-ide in newer builds
# (Windows: %USERPROFILE%\.gemini\…)
